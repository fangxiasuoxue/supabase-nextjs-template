# Refund Policy

This Refund Policy describes how we ("we", "us", "our") handle refunds and cancellations for our services.

## 1. Scope

This policy applies to services purchased directly from us, unless a separate agreement specifies different terms.

## 2. Cancellations

You may request cancellation of a service before work has commenced. Once services have been delivered or are in progress, cancellation terms depend on the stage of delivery.

## 3. Refund Eligibility

Refunds may be granted where services were not delivered as agreed. Refunds are generally not available for services that have been fully performed or for third-party costs already incurred on your behalf, such as freight or customs charges.

## 4. Requesting a Refund

To request a refund, please contact us with your account details and the reason for your request. We will review each request and respond within a reasonable time.

## 5. Processing

Approved refunds are issued using the original payment method where possible. Processing times may vary depending on your payment provider.

## 6. Contact

For questions about this Refund Policy, please contact us through the details provided in your account or agreement.
