We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with service providers who help us operate our services, and where required by law.

We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

## 5. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us.

## 6. Retention

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to provide our services and to meet our legal and business requirements.

## 7. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Notice or your data, please contact us through the details provided in your account or agreement.
